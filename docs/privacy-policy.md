# County Line — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-27_

County Line ("the app") is an Android app that notifies you when you cross from one
US county into another. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it
is handled.

## Summary

**County Line does not send your data anywhere.** All location processing happens
on your device, using a county-boundary map bundled inside the app. The app has no
servers, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Data the app accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise location** (foreground and background) | To determine which US county your device is currently in and detect when that changes | Used only on your device; **never transmitted or shared** |
| **Last-known county and your settings** (e.g. tracking on/off) | So the app can tell when you've crossed a line, and keep working after a reboot | Stored in the app's private storage on your device only |

The app requests **background location** ("Allow all the time") so it can detect
county crossings while it is closed or not in use. This is the app's core purpose.
If you grant only "While using the app", background detection is disabled and the
app tells you so.

## Data sharing and selling

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or
tracking SDKs. Your location is never sold, shared, or sent off your device.

Google Play Services' location API is used to obtain location fixes efficiently;
this is an on-device Android system component and the app does not send location
data to Google through it.

## Data retention and deletion

The last-known county and your settings persist on your device until you turn
tracking off or uninstall the app. Uninstalling County Line permanently removes
all data it stored.

## Children

County Line is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from
children under 13.
